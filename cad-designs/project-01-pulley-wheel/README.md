# Project 01: 40 mm Pulley Wheel

## Overview
A precision-designed mechanical pulley wheel featuring a 40 mm diameter footprint, a 14 mm height profile, and a central shaft bore. Modelled digitally in Tinkercad to explore rotational mechanics, power transmission components, and clean spatial geometry.

## Specifications & Dimensions
* **Outer Diameter:** 40 mm
* **Total Height:** 14 mm
* **Central Bore:** Axial through-hole designed for shaft mounting
* **Design Features:** Multi-tiered/stepped profile configured for belt routing and rotational drive applications
* **Software:** Tinkercad
* **Format:** STL / CAD Prototyped

## Engineering Principles Applied
* **Rotational Symmetry & Balance:** Ensuring concentric alignment between the outer driving surface and central bore to minimize vibration during high-speed rotation.
* **Compact Power Transmission:** Sizing a 40x40 mm footprint within a 14 mm profile to deliver efficient torque transfer in miniature mechanical assemblies.
